About the Event

Overview

CDC Conclave 2026 at IIT (ISM) Dhanbad offers a structured, cost-effective opportunity to deepen engagement with industry beyond the placement cycle.

Bringing together industry leaders, domain experts, and some of IIT (ISM) Dhanbad's brightest students, the conclave creates a powerful space for direct talent engagement, insightful conversations on emerging industry trends, and meaningful collaboration.

The two-day event focuses on Core Industries (Day 1) and Technology & Consulting (Day 2), acting as a gateway to long-term industry-academia partnerships.
